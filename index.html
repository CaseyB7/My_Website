About Us

Discover our journey, our passion, and what makes us different.

Our History

Founded in 2012 by former collegiate ballers in Chicago, Basket Ballers started out of a single garage with a simple mission: to provide everyday athletes with professional-grade gear that withstands relentless training. Over the past decade, we've grown into a nationwide brand trusted by high school standouts, collegiate players, and weekend warriors alike.

What Makes Us Different?

Unlike big-box department stores, we are 100% player-focused. Every item in our inventory—from indoor-outdoor composite basketballs to high-impact ankle braces—is tested and approved by active coaches and players. We don't just sell equipment; we live and breathe the game.
